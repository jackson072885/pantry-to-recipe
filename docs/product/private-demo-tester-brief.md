# Private Demo Tester Brief

Send this with the hosted private-demo URL.

## Message to Tester

Thanks for trying Pantry to Plate. This is a private early web demo for a small tester group.

Hosted app URL: `<paste hosted URL here>`

The app is meant to answer one dinner question:

**What can I cook tonight with what I already have?**

Please use real pantry items from your kitchen. Do not try to make the app look good. If it is confusing, slow, untrustworthy, or gives you a weak dinner idea, that is exactly what we need to learn.

## What to Try

1. Open the hosted URL in a fresh browser session if you can.
2. Add 3 to 5 ingredients you actually have right now.
3. Look for the best dinner option on Tonight.
4. Open Recommendations only if you want backup options.
5. Open one recipe that seems most useful.
6. Decide whether you would cook it tonight.

## What to Send Back

Use the feedback template in `docs/product/private-demo-feedback-template.md`, or send the same details in a message.

Most useful feedback:

- pantry items you entered
- whether the first dinner answer felt useful
- whether you trusted the top match
- what made you hesitate
- what failed, broke, or felt unclear
- device and browser if anything looked wrong

Screenshots are helpful for bugs or confusing screens.

## Demo Boundaries

- This is a private demo, not a public launch.
- There is no account or login flow in this pass.
- The app may not isolate every tester's pantry state depending on the hosting setup.
- Please avoid entering sensitive personal information.
